2. Principles

This website is deliberately built to collect as little data as possible:

  • We set no cookies and use no analytics or tracking services.
  • Fonts, images and videos are delivered locally from our own server – no connection is made to Google Fonts or other third-party CDNs.
  • No social media plug-ins are embedded.

3. Server log data

4. Contact form

When you submit the contact form, your details (company, contact person, e-mail address, optional phone number, customer status, subject and message) are transmitted to our server and forwarded from there by e-mail to info@sofatrend.sk. We process them to handle your enquiry and to initiate or conduct a business relationship (Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR) and delete them as soon as they are no longer required for this purpose and no statutory retention obligations apply.

To protect against automated spam requests, the server checks how often requests arrive from the same IP address. For this purpose the IP address is stored only as a non-reversible hash value for at most one hour and is not included in the e-mail (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR; legitimate interest in a functioning contact channel protected against misuse).

If the form cannot be sent for technical reasons, the page opens your own e-mail program with the completed details instead; you then send the message yourself.

5. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority – in Slovakia this is the Úrad na ochranu osobných údajov Slovenskej republiky.
