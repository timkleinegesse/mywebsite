Deep Free

Deep, relaxed seating with loose cushions.

Deep Free is the relaxed model range in the collection: an extra-deep seat and a pocket spring core that stays point-elastic even on generous seats. All load-bearing frame parts are solid wood.

Construction

Layer by layer.

The sectional drawing shows the positions in cross-section, the renderings the same construction on the finished piece.

Construction positions

  1. Frame
  2. Sinuous spring suspension
  3. Cover over the sinuous springs
  4. Pocket spring core with PU foam
  5. Inner cushion with filling
  6. Cover material
  7. Foot

Frame

Sturdy base frame, all load-bearing parts in solid wood.

Seat

Steel sinuous springs with cover, pocket spring core with PU foam (min. density 35), covered with textile fleece and wadding.
