Bellagio

Function model with folding armrest.

Bellagio is the function range: the armrest folds down and the seat depth adjusts in two steps. Plus a wide range of add-on elements, pointed corners, stools and cushions.

Construction

Layer by layer.

The sectional drawing shows the positions in cross-section, the renderings the same construction on the finished piece.

Construction positions

  1. Frame
  2. Sinuous spring suspension
  3. PU foam
  4. Inner cushion with filling
  5. Cover material
  6. Foot

Frame

Sturdy base frame of solid wood and wood-based materials.

Seat

Steel sinuous springs with cover, PU foam, inner cushion with filling.

Function

Folding armrest, seat depth adjustable in two steps
