Company

Craftsmanship built for series production.

sofatrend develops and manufactures upholstered suites for the furniture trade – 17 models, one shared fabric collection, production within the EU.

Profile

From Topoľčany since 2001.

In 2001 the first workshop of what is now SOFA TREND s.r.o. opened in Topoľčany, Slovakia. Since then it has grown into a manufacturer producing sofas, armchairs and beds in series – with the aim of combining craftsmanship with modern design and innovative functions.

Developed and manufactured under one roof: our own product development, our own woodworking, our own upholstery shop. We sell the result exclusively through the furniture trade.

Vertical integration

Three workshops, one house.

From the frame to the finished cover, everything is made in our own factory – which makes us independent and gives us control over every step of the production process.

01 — Woodworking

CNC-manufactured frames

CNC machines precisely process large-format wood-based materials and solid wood in-house – we do not buy in the frames of our upholstered furniture, we build them. With maximum material utilisation and as little offcut as possible.

02 — Upholstery

From spring core to seam

Construction, upholstery and cover are made in our own upholstery shop – with fabric and leather covers and functions from manual to motorised adjustment, including integrated electronics.

03 — Development

Design and engineering team

Designers and engineers develop new models together in-house – from the idea through materials and mechanics to series production. This is how ranges like Deep Free or Como come about, bringing together aesthetics, function and durability.

Sustainability

A question we ask with every decision.

How does this affect the environment, society and the economy in the long term?

Sustainability runs through all the processes of our company – from strategic decision-making to daily operations. It concerns everyone connected with us: employees, suppliers, customers and the wider public.

In practice this also means: in the product passport we point out the proper recycling of packaging material and ask that worn-out upholstered furniture be disposed of properly. A durable frame is the most effective measure of all – a piece that lasts ten years does not need replacing after four.
