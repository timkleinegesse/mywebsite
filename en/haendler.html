For retailers

A supplier that takes work off your hands.

sofatrend supplies the furniture trade only. What you get from us is not just an upholstery range but the documents your purchasing, sales and customer service teams need to work with it.

Why sofatrend

Four reasons to add us to your range.

01

Solid wood where it carries the load

On Deep Free, Modena, Como and Pisa all load-bearing frame parts are solid wood. That is not a detail for the brochure but the reason a piece of furniture is still in shape after years.

02

Pocket spring core

Seven of the 16 models sit on a point-elastic pocket spring core with PU foam from density 35 – including the extra-deep seats of the XL ranges.

03

One fabric collection for everything

Nine tested qualities with real sample photos for every colour. The same collection across all model ranges – which simplifies display and reordering.

04

Documents that do the work

Type and price lists, sectional drawings, fabric data sheet and product passport – prepared for purchasing, sales advice and complaint handling.

Frequently asked questions

What buyers ask us.

Your question is not listed? We usually reply within two working days.

Ask a question
How do I get prices and terms?

Retailers receive prices, terms and the complete type and price lists on request – personally and tailored to your store. Write to us via the contact form.

What are the lead times, are there minimum quantities?

Lead times, logistics and order quantities depend on model, cover and volume. We confirm them in the range meeting rather than promising guide values here that would not apply to your order.

Can I get fabric samples?

Yes. We provide original samples of all cover qualities and their colour palettes. As upholstery fabrics shimmer and vary between batches, the sample is the binding reference, not the screen. For a preselection, the view Fabric in a room.

How long can I reorder a model?

Reorders are possible for as long as the model is in production.

Can all models be planned in Excalibur?

Yes, the entire collection is available in the Excalibur planning software. We set up the data for your store on request.

How are complaints handled?

Complaints are handled through you as the retailer. We need detailed photos of the area in question and proof of purchase. What counts as a normal characteristic of use – such as seat shading or waves in the cover – is set out in the Product passport, which is supplied with every suite.

Is there training for our sales team?

The five modules of the sales training are available online. On-site training can be arranged on request.

Can I see the models in person?

Yes, at our showroom in Langenberg – by appointment.

Do you also sell to end customers?

No. sofatrend supplies the furniture trade only. End customers should contact the furniture store where they made their purchase.
