Quality & construction

The difference lies beneath the cover.

Two sofas can look the same and feel completely different after three years. That is why we disclose the construction of every model range.

Four layers

Frame, suspension, upholstery core, cover.

01 — Frame

Solid wood carries the load

Load-bearing frame parts in solid wood, on some ranges combined with wood-based materials. The frame decides whether a piece is still in shape after years.

02 — Suspension

Steel sinuous springs

Sinuous springs under the seat form the resilient base; a fleece layer separates steel and foam.

03 — Upholstery core

Spring core or foam

Point-elastic pocket spring core or pure foam construction, each with PU foam from density 35 and a fleece cover.

04 — Cover

One fabric collection

All model ranges use the same tested collection, every colour shown as a photo of the original fabric. To the fabric collection
