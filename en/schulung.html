Module 1

Explaining the construction – in 60 seconds.

Two sofas can look the same and feel completely different after three years. Whoever can name the layers sells the difference.

  1. Frame: carries everything – and decides whether the furniture is still square after years. On Deep Free all load-bearing parts are solid wood. The argument against the "looks the same" offer.
  2. Sinuous spring suspension: Steel sinuous springs across the seat form the resilient base. A construction-related wave pattern even when new is normal – say it before, don't explain it after.
  3. Cover over the sinuous springs: the textile fleece separating layer between steel and upholstery. Invisible but important: it prevents springs and foam from working against each other – the layer where savings are made invisibly.
  4. Pocket spring core with PU foam: the heart of the seat. Each spring works individually in its fabric pocket – point-elastic, yielding only where load is applied. The surrounding PU foam has a density of at least 35 and holds the shape.
  5. Inner cushion with filling: the loosely filled layer over the spring core creates the soft, relaxed seating feel of Deep Free. Part of its character: shake up occasionally, like a pillow.
  6. Cover material: nine tested qualities, one collection for all models – data and customer language in module 2.
  7. Foot: on Deep Free a metal foot in black or ivory, depending on the model also glides or a metal runner. Fitted only at the final location – and protects the customer's floor with felt or plastic glides.

Show on the display piece: Let customers reach under the seat – sinuous springs and spring core can be felt. The density is in the price list, not on the competitor's label.

Key sentence A

Point-elastic wins

With the pocket spring core only the spot under load yields – the person next to you stays still, the edge stays stable. Seven models sit on it: Deep Free, Deep Seat, Modena and Modular, plus Hamilton, Pure and Amalfi.

Key sentence B

Density is not firmness

Density 35 means: 35 kg of raw material per cubic metre of foam. That says something about durability and resilience, not about hard or soft. A high-quality foam can be soft – it just stays that way longer.

Key sentence C

Nobody sees the frame

Solid wood on all load-bearing parts is the reason a piece of furniture is still square after years. Anyone asked about the difference to the cheaper offer starts here.

Module 2

Translating fabric data.

The test values from the fabric data sheet are selling points – if you can translate them into customer language.

To the fabric collection

Martindale ("rubs")

Measures abrasion resistance: how often a test head rubs over the fabric until it wears through. Values from around 30,000 rubs are generally considered suitable for domestic use. The specific values for each quality are in the fabric data sheet you receive with the sample cards.

In customer language: "This fabric is tested for everyday family sofa life, not just for the living room photo."

Pilling (scale 1–5)

Rates the formation of pills after rubbing, 5 is the best value. Important: pills can also form from foreign fibres (blankets, clothing) and can be removed with a fabric shaver without damaging the fabric.

Light fastness (scale 1–8)

How much the shade fades under light – 8 would be the maximum of the blue wool scale. Values of 4–5 and higher are solid for living rooms; constant direct sun remains the enemy of every cover.

Rub fastness (scale 1–5)

Indicates whether colour transfers to clothing when rubbed – tested dry and wet. Conversely: denim abrasion on light fabric is not a fabric weakness but foreign dye.

Weight (g/running metre)

The weight shows the amount of material per running metre. Heavier qualities feel fuller and drape more richly – let customers feel the sample.

Seat shading & shimmer

Upholstery fabrics are woven in one direction. Depending on the viewing angle and light, the same fabric can appear lighter or darker – a natural effect, not a colour fault. Best shown directly on two elements standing at an angle.

The sample is binding: Colour names and screens are no substitute for the original. Batches can vary slightly – which is why every suite comes with a fabric sample in the information envelope that the customer should keep for later reorders.
Module 3

Upholstery characters and managing expectations.

Most complaints are not defects but expectations that were never discussed. This module prevents them in the sales conversation.

Character 1

Taut finish

Designed for smooth surfaces; model-related tension in the cover is part of the design. Suits customers who want things "neat".

Character 2

Loose finish

Deliberately not tightly stretched – slight waves and folds are part of the look. The line for the conversation: "That is intentional, it's called relaxed."

Character 3

Extremely relaxed upholstery

Pronounced folds, loosely fitted cover, natural look – intended by design. Whoever sells Deep Free sells this character with it.

Say it before rather than explain it after

  • Seat firmness: Curves and function elements (seat depth adjustment, sleep function) feel differently firm due to the construction. Use all seats evenly.
  • Loose cushions: can be swapped and shaken up – their advantage is even wear, their "price" the occasional patting into shape.
  • Chambered cushions: The filling sits in sewn chambers; the chamber edges may show. Shake up after use like a pillow.
  • Folds: due to the construction and part of normal use – not a defect.
  • Armrest: A design element and arm support – not a seat. Mention it kindly in the conversation.

Module 4

Demonstrate functions, don't just mention them.

Functions sell through the customer's hands. These models belong in the demonstration – each with the matching note.

To the collection

Como – seat extension

Adjustable headrests, seat extension optionally motorised, the overall depth can be extended. Note for the conversation: function parts need play – gaps are due to the construction.

Bellagio – folding armrest

Folding armrest, seat depth adjustable in two steps, plus add-on elements and stools. After the demonstration return to the starting position and plump the upholstery – this keeps the display ready for sale.

Positano – adjustable

Back adjustment and wide arm elements with lumbar cushions. Typical noises from the fittings during adjustment are normal and not a defect.

Malibu – gel foam

The additional gel foam layer can be felt – let customers compare by hand. The convertible corner turns the suite into a lying surface.

Connectors & assembly

Hook in the element connectors, let the grid connectors click audibly, fit the feet only at the final location. Showing this to the customer saves customer service a second visit.

Module 5

Complaint or typical of the product?

Complaints are handled through you as the retailer – with detailed photos of the item in question and proof of purchase. The complaint always concerns the item in question, not the entire delivery. The statutory warranty period of two years applies.

The other direction is just as important: knowing what is no a defect – and having told the customer at the time of purchase. The complete list of warranty limitations is in the product passport.

Product passport & care

Typical of the product – no grounds for complaint

  • Wave pattern with sinuous spring suspension, even when new
  • Folds and relaxed look depending on the upholstery character
  • Shimmer / seat shading depending on light and viewing angle
  • Batch-related colour and texture deviations
  • Pilling – removable with a fabric shaver
  • Dimensional deviations within the tolerances
  • Typical noises of moving function parts
  • Signs of use, pet damage, denim dye transfer, damage from unsuitable cleaning
