Soho

On metal runners, optionally with trapezoid element.

Soho stands on black metal runners and is planned in two range variants – with and without trapezoid element. Height and seat depth differ by version; the arm element is available with back depth adjustment.

Frame

Sturdy base frame, all load-bearing parts in solid wood.

Seat

Steel sinuous springs covered with textile fleece; PU foam with wadding cover.
